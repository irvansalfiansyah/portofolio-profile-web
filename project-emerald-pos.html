POS & Inventory Management App

Role: Full-Stack Developer Year: 2026

About Project

POS & Inventory Management App is a high-performance full-stack retail Point of Sale & Inventory management system, specifically designed to help retail stores easily manage sales transactions, monitor inventory levels, and track business revenue.

The application features a premium, highly responsive light/dark interface, an interactive analytical dashboard with live data visualization, and secure role-based access control (JWT-RBAC) to differentiate access rights between cashiers and managers/admins.

Key Features

  • Fast Cashier Terminal (POS): Drag-and-drop order input interface, quick search, barcode scanner support, automatic 10% VAT calculation, and instant print invoice preview.
  • Analytics Dashboard & Sales Graphs: Key KPIs including total revenue, transaction volume, average cart value, low stock warnings, and custom SVG-based interactive sales charts.
  • Inventory Management & Mutation Ledger: Dynamic stock status (Red/Yellow/Green), modal-based CRUD forms with unique SKU/Barcode validation, and comprehensive stock audit logs (manual adjustments, restocks, and sales).
  • Transaction History & One-Click Refund: Order history tracking, and a one-click refund system that automatically restores stock quantities and logs transaction cancellation details.
